About the Berkeley Scientific Journal

Founded in 1996, BSJ is UC Berkeley's premier undergraduate research journal — run entirely by undergraduates, for undergraduates and the broader scientific community.

Our Mission

Making undergraduate science visible.

"Berkeley Scientific is the premier undergraduate research journal of the University of California, Berkeley. The initial purpose of the journal was to provide an official forum in which Berkeley undergraduates could publish their independent research. Since its start in 1996, it has expanded to give the Berkeley scientific community a more in-depth understanding of itself, a broader understanding of how events here at Berkeley affect the world, and vice versa."

Every semester, our undergraduate staff publishes independent research completed by UC Berkeley undergraduates, interviews with faculty members, features articles on current issues in science, and online blog posts — spanning disciplines from ecology to engineering, astronomy to biochemistry. All research papers are faculty-reviewed, and all interviews are conducted by the staff.

BSJ is entirely student-run, from editorial direction to layout, and has been continuously publishing at UC Berkeley for nearly three decades.

How We're Organized

Five teams, one journal.

Every staff member joins one of BSJ's core departments each semester. Together, these teams research, write, edit, interview, and design each issue from the ground up.

01

Features

Long-form articles on current issues and developments across the scientific world — from emerging technology to public health — written to make complex science accessible to a general audience.

02

Interviews

Staff research and conduct in-depth interviews with UC Berkeley faculty and researchers about their work, translating cutting-edge research into engaging conversation.

03

Research & Blog

Reviews and edits original, independent research submitted by UC Berkeley undergraduates for publication, and runs BSJ's rolling digital blog — shorter pieces, opinion, and commentary on science and science policy between print issues.

04

Design

Designs and typesets every issue in InDesign — turning finished writing and art into the printed and digital publication readers see, issue after issue.

05

Copy Editing

Gives every piece — features, interviews, research, and blog posts alike — a final line-by-line pass for clarity, accuracy, and consistency before it goes to print.
